Privacy Policy

Little SHK Wonders Uploader · Effective 17 November 2026

Who uses this tool

Little SHK Wonders Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Little SHK Wonders YouTube channel, to publish that channel's own videos. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google and YouTube data it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool receives permission to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel (youtube.upload);
  • read the owner's channel details and the settings of videos it uploaded (youtube.readonly), to confirm uploads were applied correctly.

It does not read viewers' data, comments, messages, contacts or any other account.

How the data is used and stored

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and in the owner's private, encrypted server settings, so uploads can run on schedule.
  • Video IDs and upload settings are kept in the owner's private records to track what was published.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/connections. Once revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the contact details on the Little SHK Wonders YouTube channel.
