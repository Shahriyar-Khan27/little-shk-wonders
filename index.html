Little SHK Wonders Uploader

A private publishing tool for the Little SHK Wonders YouTube channel.

Little SHK Wonders Uploader is a small tool used only by the owner of the Little SHK Wonders YouTube channel. It uploads the channel's own original Shorts to that channel and reads back each upload's settings to confirm they were applied correctly.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

The tool asks Google for two permissions, and only for the owner's own channel:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload videos.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to confirm the channel and check each upload's settings.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. It is not made, endorsed or sponsored by YouTube or Google.
